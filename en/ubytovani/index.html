Included and on site

  • Filleting station for your catch
  • 15 HP boat with fish finder and electric motor
  • Shared kitchen with oven and microwave
  • Outdoor gas barbecue
  • Pool and small paddling pool
  • Parking on site

Drinking water: The camp is far from civilisation and there's no drinking water here. Please use bottled water for drinking and cooking. You can buy it in Caspe or right here at the camp, along with other drinks and coffee.

Campsite

The campsite

The camp sits among pine and olive trees close to the reservoir. There is a pool with sun loungers, a hot tub, a filleting station for cleaning your catch, a shared kitchen and a room with a fireplace, and a gas grill outside.
