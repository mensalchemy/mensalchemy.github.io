FAQ

FAQ

Didn't find your answer? Feel free to message or call us, we're happy to answer any questions.

Is a boat included in my stay?

Yes. A stay “with boat” comes with a 15 HP boat, a fish finder and a bow-mounted electric motor. In the apartment and mobile homes you can also choose a cheaper stay without a boat.

Do I need a licence to drive a boat?

Not for the 15 HP boat. For more powerful boats (30 and 50 HP), the skipper must hold a boat licence.

Is fuel paid separately?

Yes, fuel isn't included and we charge for what you actually use.

Is there drinking water at the camp?

No. The camp is far from civilisation, so you'll need bottled water for drinking and cooking. You can buy it in Caspe or right here with us.

How do I get a fishing licence?

Tick the licence option when booking and send us a copy of your ID no later than 14 days before arrival. The licence costs €20 per year; the coto permit is €40 per week or €8 per day.

Can I cook for myself?

Yes. The accommodation has small kitchenettes, and there's a shared kitchen with an oven and microwave plus a gas barbecue on site. On request we also offer half board for €25 per person per day.

What do I pay extra?

Cleaning, including boat cleaning, at €20 per person per stay, fuel by actual use, and your licence and permit.

What bites when?

You can fish all year. Zander are best from October to May; wels catfish, carp and black bass from May to October.

How do the deposit and balance work?

You pay a deposit when booking and the balance no later than 30 days before arrival. You can pay by bank transfer.

Where can I go shopping?

In Caspe, about 15 minutes by car. There are shops, a chemist, a pharmacy and a medical emergency centre.

Is there a pool at the camp?

Yes, a pool and a small paddling pool to cool off on hot days.

Can I fish for catfish from the bank on the river?

Yes, we can arrange a trip further up the river. You bring your own gear and sleep out in nature on the bank. You'll find the prices in the price list.
