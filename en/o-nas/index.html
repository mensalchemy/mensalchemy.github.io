About us

Cuesta Falcon is a family camp run by Peťa and Vašek, and you can tell they put their heart into it.

Our story

We first came to the Ebro as ordinary anglers. Like so many others, we fell for the zander, the catfish and the sunsets over the water.

Bit by bit, we made our dream of our own camp come true, roughly halfway along the Mequinenza reservoir. We built a lot of it ourselves, and every winter we add something new.

We make our own lures and jigs and love sharing what we've learnt. What makes us happiest is when guests come back.
